Protected local recovery

Progress is encrypted with AES-GCM before being stored in this browser. This protects against casual local inspection but is not a substitute for authenticated server-side records, secure backups, or account recovery.

Production security controls checklist

  • Passkeys or phishing-resistant MFA with step-up authentication.
  • Server-side authorization on every course, assessment, and record operation.
  • Short-lived sessions, secure cookies, CSRF protection, and session revocation.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest with managed key rotation.
  • Append-only audit logs, anomaly detection, alerting, and tested backups.
  • Input validation, output encoding, dependency scanning, SAST/DAST, and penetration testing.
  • Least-privilege access, separation of duties, and reviewed administrative actions.
  • Privacy retention rules, incident response, disaster recovery, and accessibility testing.
