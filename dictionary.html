Academic Dictionary

Explore helpful definitions for common university, technology, business, and design terms.

Academic

Curriculum

The organized set of courses, learning objectives, and educational experiences offered by an institution.

Academic

Semester

A defined period of the academic year during which classes and assessments take place.

Technology

Algorithm

A step-by-step set of instructions used to solve a problem or complete a computational task.

Technology

Artificial Intelligence

Computer-based systems designed to perform tasks that commonly require human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns or generating responses.

Business

Entrepreneurship

The process of developing, launching, and managing a venture or new business idea.

Business

Revenue

The income a business receives from its normal activities, such as selling products or services.

Design

User Experience (UX)

The overall experience a person has when interacting with a product, service, or digital interface.

Design

Typography

The practice of arranging type to make written language readable, accessible, and visually effective.
