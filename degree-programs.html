Adopted undergraduate credit standard

Bonds University will use a proposed common planning standard of 128 semester credits, normally 32 courses × 4 credits, for every listed bachelor's degree program.

Comparison: Harvard College requires 128 credits, normally 32 four-credit courses. Yale College requires 36 term courses and does not use credit hours, so Yale's course count is not directly convertible without an institutional equivalency policy. Stanford requires 180 quarter units, which uses a different academic calendar and unit system. The Bonds standard is therefore a planning convention informed by the comparison—not a claim of equivalency, accreditation, or affiliation.
