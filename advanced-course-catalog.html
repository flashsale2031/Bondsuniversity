Course design standard

1. Pre-assessment

Diagnostic questions identify prior knowledge, misconceptions, and readiness.

2. Orientation

Learning objectives, required tools, study plan, academic integrity, and support routes.

3. Focused lectures

Short lessons with examples, terminology, demonstrations, and accessible study resources.

4. Scenario practice

Realistic case studies require learners to interpret evidence and make justified decisions.

5. Section testing

Frequent knowledge checks provide feedback and identify topics needing review.

6. Progress review

Saved activity, reflection prompts, and instructor feedback guide the next learning step.

7. Final examination

Validated summative assessment with identity, integrity, grading, and appeal controls.

8. Completion review

Faculty-approved completion records and certificates only after institutional authorization.
