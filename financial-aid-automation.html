Automated 50/50 Student Funding

This workflow is designed to help the university verify government financial-aid awards and calculate a matching institutional credit of the same verified amount, subject to eligibility, cost-of-attendance limits, funding rules, and authorized review.

Institutional match = verified eligible government aid × 1.00, subject to applicable limits and approvals.
1

Verify the award

Import award data only from an authorized government or approved aid-system source. Validate student identity, enrollment, term, award type, amount, and status.

2

Check eligibility

Confirm enrollment, satisfactory academic progress, program eligibility, cost of attendance, duplicate funding, and any applicable federal, state, or institutional restrictions.

3

Calculate the match

Calculate a provisional institutional credit equal to the verified eligible government amount. Apply caps and prevent over-awards before any posting.

4

Approve and audit

Route exceptions to authorized financial-aid staff, record every decision, require separation of duties, and preserve a complete audit trail.

5

Credit the student

After approval and reconciliation, post the institutional credit to the student ledger. Keep disbursement on hold when verification fails or documentation is incomplete.

6

Reconcile continuously

Compare government records, university ledger entries, refunds, withdrawals, and adjustments. Flag mismatches and create correction tasks.

Required control checks

  • Government award source and timestamp are recorded.
  • Student consent, privacy, and access permissions are enforced.
  • Government and institutional funds are tracked separately.
  • Over-award, duplicate-award, and cost-of-attendance rules are checked.
  • Human review is required for exceptions, reversals, and refunds.
  • Accreditation, Title IV, state, tax, and institutional policies are reviewed before production use.

Important implementation boundary

This page is a planning prototype, not an accredited financial-aid system and does not move money or certify government compliance. A production implementation requires approved data integrations, security controls, legal and financial-aid review, documented institutional policy, testing, and authorization from the applicable agencies.
