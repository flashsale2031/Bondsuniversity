# Financial Aid Incident Response Plan

## Trigger events
Unauthorized access, suspected disclosure, incorrect award, duplicate credit, fraudulent application, unavailable agency connection, lost device, or compromised credential.

## Response steps
1. Detect and record the time, system, user, and indicators.
2. Triage severity and notify the incident commander.
3. Contain access or processing without destroying evidence.
4. Preserve logs, records, messages, and relevant system snapshots.
5. Notify legal, privacy, security, financial aid, and executive contacts.
6. Determine agency, student, regulator, and contractual notifications with counsel.
7. Eradicate the cause, restore safely, and validate ledger integrity.
8. Document root cause, affected records, decisions, notifications, and corrective actions.
9. Complete a post-incident review and track remediation to closure.
