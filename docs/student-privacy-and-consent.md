# Student Privacy and Electronic Consent Template

**Draft for counsel and privacy officer review**

Before submitting an application, the student should receive a clear notice describing:
- What information is collected and why.
- Which agency, institution, or service provider will receive it.
- Whether each disclosure is required, permitted, or optional.
- How records are secured, retained, corrected, and deleted when legally permitted.
- How to request access, correction, a paper copy, or assistance.
- Contact details for privacy questions and complaints.
