# Bonds University Financial Aid Compliance Documentation Pack

**Status:** Draft templates for legal, compliance, security, and agency review. **Not an approval or certification.**

## 1. Required governance decisions
- Identify the jurisdiction(s), funding programs, and participating agencies.
- Confirm institutional eligibility, authorization, accreditation, and program eligibility before accepting funds.
- Appoint a financial-aid administrator and an information-security coordinator.
- Approve a written information-security program, risk assessment, incident response plan, records schedule, and vendor oversight process.
- Define who may approve awards, release credits, reverse credits, reconcile funds, and communicate with agencies.

## 2. Core evidence file
Maintain controlled versions of:
- Institutional authorization, accreditation, licenses, and program approvals.
- Applicable agency agreements, participation agreements, SAIG or equivalent agreements, and system-access approvals.
- Policies for eligibility, satisfactory academic progress, cost of attendance, disbursement, refunds, returns, over-awards, and reconciliation.
- Security risk assessment, data inventory, data-flow diagrams, access matrix, penetration-test summary, vulnerability remediation log, and business-continuity test results.
- Vendor due diligence, contracts, data-processing terms, breach obligations, subcontractor list, and security attestations.
- Training records, background checks where required, annual reviews, internal audits, and corrective-action plans.

## 3. Approval gates
1. Legal review of authority to participate and collect/process information.
2. Compliance review against each agency's current rules.
3. Security review of architecture, identity proofing, MFA, encryption, logging, backups, and incident response.
4. Privacy review covering FERPA, applicable state privacy law, GLBA, tax-information restrictions, and data minimization.
5. Finance review of ledger controls, segregation of duties, reconciliation, and refund/return processes.
6. Executive sign-off followed by a limited pilot and independent validation.

## 4. Change control
Every production change must include an owner, risk rating, test evidence, approval, rollback plan, implementation date, and post-change validation.

## 5. Source baseline
For U.S. Title IV participation, consult the current Federal Student Aid Handbook, applicable regulations, the institution's participation agreements, and agency-specific guidance. Requirements vary by program and jurisdiction.
